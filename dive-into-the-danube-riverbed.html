2025 · Interactive audiovisual installation

Dive into the Danube Riverbed

Ceramics: Jacob Bartmann
Music and electronics: Zekő Sebesy

Dive into the Danube Riverbed is an interactive installation that translates sonar measurements of the Danube into sound and image. Visitors navigate along the river through a series of ceramic objects and encounter the changing form of its riverbed as an audiovisual landscape.

Buttons move the virtual position upstream and downstream, while water level and light sensors reshape the musical material in real time. The work brings together ceramics, environmental data, physical interaction and generative electronics.

The installation was presented at Ars Electronica 2025 in Linz as part of Arts of Change — Change of Arts.
